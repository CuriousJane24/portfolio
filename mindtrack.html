MindTrack: Helping New Users Get Started

Mock Customer Success case study · Built in HubSpot

Product and scenario

MindTrack is presented in this mock scenario as a tool for tracking work sessions and understanding where working hours go.

The onboarding challenge was to help new users move from signing up to logging their first meaningful work session.

My approach

I built a four-email onboarding sequence in HubSpot, combining welcome guidance, reminders, an illustrative use case and a personal check-in.

The project set a target of a first session within 10 days. This was a design target, not a measured result.

The email sequence

  1. Welcome: introduce MindTrack and guide the user towards logging a first session.
  2. Follow-up: explain how tracking sessions helps users understand where their hours go.
  3. Illustrative story: demonstrate how time tracking could inform a freelancer’s pricing decisions.
  4. Check-in: ask whether the user needs help getting started.

What the evidence demonstrates

The project screenshot shows four configured email steps and business-day delays in HubSpot. The visible email statistics show zero sends.

This demonstrates sequence setup and onboarding communication design. It does not establish a measured reduction in drop-off.
