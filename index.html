About Me

Since founding Madamereal Fabrics & Styling in 2018, I’ve managed customer relationships across the full order journey, from the first consultation through delivery and follow-up. I coordinated orders, handled service recovery and used customer feedback to improve how we worked. That experience shaped how I manage expectations and follow through on commitments.

I bring that same approach to Customer Success, CRM and Operations. My work focuses on onboarding, product adoption and retention. I use HubSpot to build onboarding workflows, track customer health and flag changes in product usage so the team knows when to reach out and what support to offer.

What I Focus On

  • CRM data quality. I clean up duplicate contacts so the team can rely on the customer records it uses for follow-up.
  • Time to first value. I design onboarding around one meaningful first step, like helping a new MindTrack user log their first work session.
  • Service recovery. When an order goes wrong, I work with the customer to understand the issue, coordinate a resolution and use what I learn to improve the process.
