Madamereal: Customer Service Recovery

A high value remote customer’s custom dress didn’t fit, and her event was two weeks away. As founder of Madamereal Fabrics & Styling,I coordinated the remake and express delivery. The experience also changed how I onboard customers who order remotely.

My role: Founder, Madamereal Fabrics & Styling
Focus: Service recovery, customer communication and onboarding
Remake turnaround: 48–72 hours

1. The customer’s concern

We had made the dress using measurements the customer sent us. When she tried it on, she contacted us about the fit. With her event two weeks away, I needed to understand what was wrong and work out how we could get a replacement to her in time.

2. How I handled the recovery

  1. Confirmed the issue. I arranged a video call so the customer could show me how the dress fit. Seeing it on her helped me understand what needed to change.
  2. Coordinated the remake. I worked with the team to make a replacement. We completed it within 48–72 hours.
  3. Arranged express delivery. Once the replacement was ready, we sent it by express shipping so it could reach her before the event.

3. What changed in customer onboarding

Before this order, I relied on measurements sent by remote customers. The video call showed me what an initial consultation could catch before we started making a dress.

I changed our process so remote orders begin with a virtual consultation. I use that conversation to clarify what the customer wants and guide her through taking the measurements. We can now discuss questions about fit while there is still time to adjust the order before production.

4. The outcome

The replacement reached the customer in time for her event and it fit. The lasting change for Madamereal was moving that conversation about fit to the start of the customer journey.

Reduced fit Problem from 75% to 20% in 90 days - 3Months ( -73% Churn indicators ).

Referral from customer increased by 6+ persons.

100% on Time resolution & Preserving critical client relationship.
