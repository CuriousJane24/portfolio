Customer Experience Case Study

Ijeoma Home & Living: Unifying the Omnichannel Customer Journey

How cross-functional coordination, AI-driven visual alignment, and Zoho CRM automation rescued post-purchase satisfaction for a 9,200+ account furniture manufacturer.

My Role: Quality Assurance, Team Coordination & Automation Architecture
Core Competencies: Omnichannel Alignment, Customer Journey Mapping, Project Management
Tool Focus: Zoho CRM Automation & AI Video Modeling

1. The Fragmentation Gap (Situation & Task)

Ijeoma Home & Living is a growing mid-market furniture enterprise managing custom, made-to-order manufacturing across three physical showrooms and an expanding online storefront. Despite servicing a substantial base of over 9,200 customers with a highly rated physical product, severe operational friction points threatened their retention margins and brand reputation.

A comprehensive analysis of client feedback and support tickets exposed critical, structural post-purchase vulnerabilities:

  • Pricing Discrepancies: Customers were presented with conflicting quotes by online and showroom representatives, with no centralized data validation.
  • Onboarding Communication Delay: Online inquiries frequently suffered from a 48-hour response lag, shifting clients from excited to anxious.
  • Zero Transparency Lifecycle: High-value payments were processed without immediate written order confirmations, leaving clients guessing if their customization specs were understood.
  • The Production Black Box: Customers received zero status check-ins during multi-week manufacturing phases or proactive communication around installation and post-purchase follow-up.

This data clear signaled that operational misalignment was shifting customer sentiment from excitement to anxiety, frustration, and doubt. The task was to unify the physical and digital channels into a clear, transparent customer success roadmap.

2. Strategic Cross-Functional Execution (Action)

To eliminate this friction, I served as a central bridge across the Quality Assurance, Creative, and Operations teams, implementing an integrated overhaul across three key focus regions:

⚙️ Unified Operational Roadmap:

  • Cross-Functional Project Management: Facilitated alignment syncs, collected localized suggestions, and managed accountability loops for inactive teammates to ensure full organizational adoption of the new customer success journey.
  • AI-Driven Visual Illustrative Models: Coordinated with the creative team to engineer custom AI video walkthroughs, visually illustrating the explicit step-by-step custom furniture creation cycle to manage customer expectations and lower post-purchase anxiety.
  • Zoho CRM Lifecycle Automation Architecture: Reviewed and re-mapped the automated communication structures within Zoho CRM to guarantee instant, clear email order confirmations, transparent milestone statuses, and win-back logic sequences.

3. Re-Engineering the Order-to-Delivery Pipeline

We completely restructured the communication touchpoints, making automated transparency standard at every milestone:

By defining explicit data properties within Zoho, any order finalized in-store or online instantly locks quotes site-wide to completely remove pricing friction. Concurrently, trigger sequences track the exact phases of factory assembly, automatically delivering milestone notifications directly to the client's inbox to guarantee absolute clarity throughout the process.

4. Quantitative Impact & Value (Results)

Transitioning Ijeoma Home & Living away from siloed communication loops into an automated, synchronized process completely stabilized their customer satisfaction metrics:

100% Quote Alignment eliminating cross-channel customer friction
Reduced Communication Dead Zones via proactive milestone tracking updates
Scalable Team Accountability tracking account touchpoints transparently
