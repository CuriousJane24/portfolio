Madamereal: Customer Service Recovery

Real-world founder experience

The challenge

A customer ordered a custom dress remotely and she sent in her measurements/size. After delivery, the dress did not fit and her event was 14 days away.

How I responded

  • Used a video call with the customer to clarify the fit issue while acknowledging her disappointment.
  • Coordinated with the team to source exact fabric & remake the dress within 48–72 hours.
  • Arranged express shipping to reach the customer before her event.

The outcome

  • The replacement arrived in time for the event ,customer was surprised, fit was perfect and customer was happy.
  • This increased trust with the customer, 6 more referral from her.
  • Reduced fit complain from 75% to 20% in 3months.

What I learned and changed

This experience taught me to start with a consultation so customers understand our requirements, rather than relying solely on measurements they send.

I stopped accepting customer-supplied measurements on their own and began guiding clients through virtual measurements during consultations.
