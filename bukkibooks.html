BukkiBooks: Detecting Customer Disengagement

Digital Witch Group.

My role: Project Manager, Research Team Member and Automation Team Member.

The scenario

BukkiBooks was presented as cloud accounting and inventory software serving SMEs. In the case scenario, Ihuoma Fashion House, one of its Customers stopped using key features for 5 weeks.

Customer outreach revealed that a new manager had not been trained. Rather than raising a complaint, the manager returned to manual spreadsheets.

My contribution

  • Managed the group project and contributed to the research and automation teams.
  • Researched Nigerian fintech and SaaS companies, their operations and key metrics.
  • Built a five-stage Customer Success process covering onboarding through retention.
  • Built a customer health score and priority and at-risk workflows designed to identify disengagement early.

Customer health model

The model combined 4 components into a score out of 100:

  • Login and product activity: 30%.
  • Product and feature adoption: 30%.
  • Transaction volume: 25%.
  • Customer support activity: 15%.

Health bands: 80–100 Healthy💚, 50–79 Neutral🧡, and 0–49 At Risk❤️.

Segmentation and prioritisation

The project used health status, seven-day inactivity, 30-day dormancy, unresolved support tickets and subscription tier to segment and prioritise customers.

These were project-specific rules designed to support earlier investigation and follow-up.

Tools and project evidence

HubSpot, Trello, Slack, Outlook, Google Sheets, Google Meet and Canva.

This cohort project demonstrates process design, customer health modelling,Adoption,Retention and automation work. No measured churn reduction is claimed.
